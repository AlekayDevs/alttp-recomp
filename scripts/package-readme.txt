ALttP Recomp - The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (USA), recompiled to native code
=======================================================================================

ENGLISH
1. Double-click alttp-recomp.exe.
2. Choose YOUR copy of the ROM (USA, CRC32 777AAC2F; .sfc or .smc). It is not included and never leaves your computer.
3. Press Play. The first time it builds the game from your ROM (a few minutes); the next starts are instant.

Saves and save states go to the "saves" folder next to alttp-recomp.exe.
If Windows SmartScreen or your antivirus complains: the program is not code-signed and it compiles code on your PC
(everything is local). See docs/FAQ.md on the project page for the steps.
Help, source code and downloads: https://github.com/ArgoxDevs/alttp-recomp

ESPANOL
1. Haz doble clic en alttp-recomp.exe.
2. Elige TU copia de la ROM (USA, CRC32 777AAC2F; .sfc o .smc). No se incluye y nunca sale de tu equipo.
3. Pulsa Jugar. La primera vez construye el juego desde tu ROM (unos minutos); los siguientes arranques son instantaneos.

Las partidas y los estados van a la carpeta "saves" junto a alttp-recomp.exe.
Si Windows SmartScreen o tu antivirus protestan: el programa no esta firmado y compila codigo en tu PC (todo es local).
Mira docs/FAQ.es.md en la pagina del proyecto.
Ayuda, codigo y descargas: https://github.com/ArgoxDevs/alttp-recomp
